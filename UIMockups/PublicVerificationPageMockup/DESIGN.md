## Brand & Style

This design system delivers a civic editorial aesthetic crafted for organised labor in the digital economy. The visual ethos combines the historical gravitas of collective solidarity with the clean, utilitarian ergonomics of modern public services. The interface must inspire institutional trust, clarity, dignity, and collective purpose among gig and platform workers across logistics, transport, food delivery, and digital services.

Drawing from modern civic editorial design, the system rejects commercial gimmickry, low-contrast micro-copy, and playful consumer tech trends. Instead, it deploys disciplined hierarchy, hairline structural framing, generous typographic whitespace, and deliberate accents of deep ideological crimson. Interactions are decisive, tactile, and frictionless, ensuring usability under challenging physical field conditions—direct sunlight, budget smartphones, one-handed grip, and rapid transit scenarios.

## Colors

The palette balances historical labor symbolism with clinical civic legibility:

- **Primary (`#C81D25`)**: Deep ideological crimson. Used intentionally for principal actions, primary alerts, institutional affiliation insignias, active status indicators, and key metric counters. Never diluted with gradients.
- **Secondary (`#4B5563`)**: Cool muted slate. Applied to secondary narrative copy, metadata labels, structural icons, and supplementary navigation items.
- **Tertiary (`#1E3A8A`)**: Civic navy. Reserved exclusively for institutional validation, legal disclaimers, ratified agreements, and statutory labor compliance states.
- **Neutral Dark (`#111827`)**: Deep charcoal slate. The bedrock text color across headlines and body content, avoiding harsh pitch-black for optimal optical comfort.
- **Background Base (`#FAFAFA`)**: Neutral off-white canvas that softens screen glare during extended reading.
- **Surface Elevation (`#FFFFFF`)**: Pure white cards providing crisp optical contrast against the canvas.
- **Border Grayscale (`#E5E7EB`)**: Precise 1px hairline boundary color for tabular rows, inputs, and card perimeters.
- **Muted Fill (`#F3F4F6`)**: Low-contrast background for badges, inactive track toggles, and metadata chips.

## Typography

The typographic system relies on an authoritative, accessible bilingual pairing: Inter for English and Latin numerical glyphs, paired seamlessly with Noto Sans Malayalam. Both families share optical balance, vertical proportions, and open counters that preserve clear differentiation in low-resolution screens or dense script forms.

When rendering Malayalam text:
- Increase line height by 8–12% relative to Latin baseline definitions to avoid clipping conjunct glyphs (chillaksharangal) and ascender marks.
- Maintain consistent optical weights across both scripts (`400` Regular for legal terms, manifesto tracts, and notifications; `600` Semibold and `700` Bold for section titles and official action alerts).
- Never render Malayalam body or headline copy in italics or oblique weights.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is anchored by an 8-point geometric grid configured as a structured, responsive column matrix:

- **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12-column layout with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters, maximum content container width of 1200px, and outer margins of 32px (`2rem`).
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px)**: 8-column layout with 20px gutters and 24px outer margins.
- **Mobile (320px – 767px)**: 4-column layout with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and 16px (`1rem`) safe margins.

All component spacing adheres to strict structural intervals: `space-xs` (4px) for micro badge padding and icon-to-label gaps; `space-sm` (8px) for list element separation and inline button internals; `space-md` (16px) for interior card padding and form field vertical stacks; `space-lg` (24px) for card-to-card separation and header locks; `space-xl` (40px) for editorial section breaks and public declaration blocks.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is communicated via clean structural framing and hairline separation rather than heavy skeuomorphism:

- **Surface Layers**: The `#FAFAFA` document canvas serves as Level 0. Work surfaces, dispatch tables, grievance forms, and editorial announcements sit on Level 1 pure white (`#FFFFFF`) containers.
- **Borders & Outlines**: Every container, input surface, and modal window utilizes a crisp 1px solid hairline border in `#E5E7EB`.
- **Shadow Profile**: Shadows are deliberately subdued, quiet, and sharp. Level 1 elements feature a delicate ambient drop: `0px 1px 3px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.05), 0px 1px 2px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.03)`. Floating sheets, sticky grievance action bars, and modal dialogues employ Level 2 elevation: `0px 10px 15px -3px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.08), 0px 4px 6px -2px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.03)`.
- No colored glows, dramatic blurs, or tinted shadows are permitted. Depth remains restrained and institutional.

## Shapes

The design system uses a restrained corner treatment: Level 1 (Soft). 

- Base UI containers, cards, tables, text areas, input fields, and action buttons utilize a disciplined 4px radius (`0.25rem`). This preserves an authoritative, structured, and institutional document feel.
- Medium interactive elements and alert banners scale to 8px radius (`0.5rem`).
- Modal sheets and floating persistent taskbars utilize 12px radius (`0.75rem`).
- **Intentional Exception (Badges & Chips)**: Status badges, category indicators, and count trackers use full pill roundness (`9999px`) to create optical contrast against the strictly rectangular cards and buttons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Solid `#C81D25` background, `#FFFFFF` text, 4px border radius. Minimum height 48px to accommodate mobile touch targets under high vibration or outdoor transit conditions. Hover: `#A5161D`. Focus: 2px solid `#C81D25` outline with 2px offset.
- **Secondary Civic**: Solid `#FFFFFF` background, 1px solid `#E5E7EB` border, `#111827` text. Hover: `#F9FAFB` fill, `#D1D5DB` border.
- **Tertiary / Destructive**: Outline or ghost treatment with unambiguous semantic red labeling for dispute escalation or strike call registers.

### Badges & Status Chips
- Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), optical padding `4px 12px`.
- **Active / Official**: Solid `#FEE2E2` background with `#991B1B` text.
- **Verified Worker / Statutory**: Solid `#EFF6FF` background with `#1E40AF` text.
- **Neutral / Informational**: Solid `#F3F4F6` background with `#374151` text.

### Cards & Editorial Panels
- Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) surface, 1px solid `#E5E7EB` hairline framing, 4px corner radius, and Level 1 soft shadow.
- Interior padding strictly fixed at 20px (mobile) and 24px (desktop).
- Header sections within cards include hairline bottom dividers (`#F3F4F6`) when separating metadata from narrative grievance details.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Background: `#FFFFFF`. Border: 1px solid `#D1D5DB`. Border radius: 4px.
- Height: 44px minimum for standard fields; text size 15px with `#111827` value text and `#9CA3AF` placeholder.
- Focus state: Border shifts to `#C81D25` with an immediate zero-blur 1px outer ring in `#C81D25`. No rounded blue browser defaults.
- Labels are strictly stacked top-left, rendered in `label-lg` with `body-sm` helper copy underneath.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Base: 20px × 20px square (checkbox) or circle (radio), 1.5px border `#9CA3AF`.
- Active State: Filled `#C81D25` with pure white checkmark icon or concentric white dot.

### Specialized Union Platform Components
- **Union Membership Verification Bar**: High-prominence horizontal panel displaying worker ID, local unit (Ernakulam, Thiruvananthapuram, Kozhikode, etc.), digital membership seal, and verified payment badge.
- **Bilingual Language Switcher**: Persistent, high-contrast segmented control (`English` | `മലയാളം`) fixed within the upper utility header, adhering to 4px container radius with solid crimson active tab fill.
- **Grievance Ticket Tracker**: Stepped hairline progress timeline utilizing `#C81D25` nodes for active stages, `#10B981` for resolved claims, and `#E5E7EB` for pending union legal interventions.
